Sierra Leone Money Trail

A KaiKai News public-interest reporting series in preparation, focused on verifiable evidence about trafficking, illicit-finance risks and public accountability in Sierra Leone.

This page contains no claims against named people and no assertion of an international investigative partnership.

Separate areas of inquiry

Cocaine: published court decisions, maritime trafficking documentation and attributable investigative reporting.

Kush: independent public-health research, supply-chain reporting and humanitarian impacts.

Public finance: traceable official records, not assumptions based on association or photographs.

Each published story must link its sources and distinguish what remains alleged or unknown.

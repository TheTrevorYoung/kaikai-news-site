OUR METHODS

Evidence first. Fairness always.

Claim labels

  • Verified: substantiated by authentic records or sufficient independent evidence.
  • Reported: properly attributed to another source, without claiming independent confirmation.
  • Alleged: a disputed or unresolved claim, not a conviction or finding.
  • Unknown: no defensible conclusion yet.

Before publication

We verify documents, distinguish charges from convictions, seek responses to material allegations, inspect sourcing and permissions, and check privacy and source safety. Cocaine trafficking and Kush networks are separate reporting tracks unless evidence establishes a connection.
